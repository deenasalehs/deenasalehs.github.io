About Me Research CV

I am Dr. Deena Saleh, also known as Deena Saleh Şahin, an Egyptian Turkish political economist studying social capital, trust, and economic sociology in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA). I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from Istanbul University (2023), an M.Sc. in Economics from Hacettepe University, and a B.Sc. in General Management Technology from the German University in Cairo (GUC); major of HRM and a minor of International Business; along with a Micro Masters in Digital Communications and Marketing from Istanbul University. My scholarship specializes in political economy, cultural economics, and applied quantitative methods, with a primary focus on the MENA region.
